NIOT Technical Feasibility Guidelines (Carnot Cycle Analysis)

NIOT / Ministry of Earth Sciences

Commercial OTEC operations necessitate a year-round temperature differential ΔT ≥ 20.0°C between surface ocean water (28–31°C) and deep ocean water at 1,000 meters depth (5–7°C). Closed-cycle plants vaporize ammonia working fluid, while open-cycle systems generate desalinated fresh water as a co-benefit. Sites within 100–350 km of coastlines minimize submarine power transmission cable expenditures.
